Summer Series 2026

Connect. Unwind. Have Fun. ♡

Three unforgettable events. One incredible summer.

This summer, we're inviting educators from across the UK to step away from the classroom and experience something different.

Whether you're looking to challenge yourself, celebrate a special milestone, or simply enjoy a fun day with like-minded people, our Teachers Connect Summer Series has something for everyone.

Across three unique events, you'll have the opportunity to connect with educators from all backgrounds, create lasting memories, and be part of a community that believes teachers deserve opportunities to unwind, socialise, and enjoy life outside of school.

This year is even more special as we celebrate 2 years of Teachers Connect, and we can't wait to celebrate this milestone with you.
